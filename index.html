Engineering & CA Capabilities

🇮🇳
Indian Banking Formats Multi-Bank Normalization

Automatic regex & column detection for HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Kotak, and Canara Bank statements. Supports credit/debit inversion, unified narration, and reference extraction.

⚖️
Rule-of-9 Diagnostic Automated Transposition Audit

Mathematical transposition verification: whenever ledger variance divides by 9, algorithmic pattern matching inspects transposed digits (e.g. ₹270 vs ₹450) and flags them for rapid auditor review.

📑
ICAI Statutory BRS Auditor-Ready Print & PDF

Generates formal Bank Reconciliation Statement starting from Cash Book closing balance, adding cheques issued not presented, and deducting uncredited deposits to precisely balance Passbook.

🔒
Air-Gapped Execution 100% In-Browser Privacy

No server ingestion, no cloud databases, zero telemetric logging. All ledger parsing and matching occurs strictly in local browser JavaScript memory.
